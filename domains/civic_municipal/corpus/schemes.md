# Government Scheme Discovery — Karnataka

These are three of Karnataka's current welfare "guarantee" schemes, chosen because they are high-volume, frequently asked-about, and have a reasonable amount of independently corroborated detail available. All three are delivered primarily through the Seva Sindhu Guarantee Schemes portal and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to a bank account, which is a pattern worth stating plainly to a caller: these schemes generally pay into a linked bank account rather than issuing cash or vouchers at an office.

## Gruha Lakshmi (monthly cash transfer to women heads of household)

Gruha Lakshmi provides **₹2,000 per month** by Direct Benefit Transfer to the woman recorded as the head of family on a Below Poverty Line (BPL), Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY), or Above Poverty Line (APL) ration card. The scheme, run by Karnataka's Department of Women and Child Development, began disbursing payments from 16 August 2023. Eligibility as reported: the applicant must be at least 18 years old, a Karnataka resident, and hold a ration card in her name as head of household — there is **no upper age limit** and no requirement that she be married; unmarried, widowed, divorced, or separated women listed as head of household qualify. The applicant's Aadhaar, bank account, and ration card names should match. **Exclusions**, per the same sources: women employed by central government, state government, or a public sector undertaking; women whose family members are income-tax payers; and women already receiving other government pension benefits above a specified threshold are not eligible, to avoid duplicate benefits. Source: independently published Karnataka-schemes guides cross-referencing the Department of Women and Child Development's public eligibility criteria.

As of mid-2026, the state government announced that all beneficiaries must **re-apply** for verification purposes, following reports of deceased or ineligible (e.g. income-tax-paying) individuals continuing to receive payments; a one-time biometric re-verification was being rolled out across roughly 17,000 kiosks. This is a live, evolving process as of the last-verified date above — a caller asking about Gruha Lakshmi status in 2026 should be told re-verification may be required, and directed to check current status via the Seva Sindhu Guarantee Schemes portal (sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in) rather than given a fixed answer, since the process was actively changing at time of writing.

## Anna Bhagya (free/subsidized food grain)

Anna Bhagya is Karnataka's food-security scheme providing **10 kg of rice per person per month** to eligible (BPL/AAY) households — structured as 5 kg under the central Nationa Food Security Act (NFSA) allocation plus an additional 5 kg under the state's own Anna Bhagya top-up. Where physical rice is not available for distribution, the scheme provides a cash equivalent via DBT, reported at **₹170 per person per month**. Source: independently published Karnataka government-schemes guides, cross-checked against the general NFSA + state-top-up structure that is consistent with how Anna Bhagya has been publicly described. **UNVERIFIED — needs confirmation:** the exact current cash-equivalent rate (₹170/person/month) should be checked against the live Food, Civil Supplies and Consumer Affairs Department notification before being quoted as current, since these top-up rates are subject to periodic government revision.

## Gruha Jyothi (free household electricity)

Gruha Jyothi provides **free electricity up to 200 units per month** for eligible domestic consumers, delivered through the local ESCOM (for Bengaluru, this is BESCOM) rather than through a separate application portal — sources describe it as automatically applied to the electricity bill for eligible connections rather than requiring a standalone application in the way Gruha Lakshmi does. Status can reportedly be checked at the Seva Sindhu Guarantee Schemes portal (sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in), or via SMS by sending the 12-digit ration card number to 8147500500 or 8277000555. Source: independently published Karnataka-schemes guides. **UNVERIFIED — needs confirmation:** the precise eligibility formula (e.g., whether it is capped strictly at consumption ≤200 units or phases out above a threshold) was not independently confirmed against an official BESCOM/ESCOM notification during this research.

## A general caution for this category

Nearly all sourcing for Karnataka's welfare-guarantee schemes in this file comes from independently published citizen-guide sites rather than a first-party government notification retrieved directly, because official scheme-detail pages were not directly accessible during this research session. This is disclosed rather than smoothed over: **before this content is used to state a firm eligibility rule or a payment amount to a real citizen, it should be cross-checked against the Department of Women and Child Development's and BESCOM's own current notifications.** Where schemes and eligibility rules conflict between sources, or a citizen's situation is genuinely borderline (e.g., unclear ration-card category, disputed head-of-household status), this is exactly the kind of case that should route to a human, not be resolved by the assistant's own interpretation — consistent with the safety restriction that AI-generated interpretation must be clearly separated from verified information.
