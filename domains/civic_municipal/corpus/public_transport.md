# Public Transport Assistance — Bengaluru (BMTC & Namma Metro)

## BMTC (city buses)

Bengaluru Metropolitan Transport Corporation (BMTC) operates the city's bus network. Monthly pass rates, following a revision effective **January 2026**, are reported as: **₹1,200** for the Ordinary Monthly Pass, **₹2,000** for the Vajra (AC) Monthly Pass, and **₹4,000** for the Vayu Vajra Gold Monthly Pass, which covers all routes including the airport express service. Passes can be obtained at BMTC depots, authorised outlets, or through the Namma BMTC mobile app, which also provides real-time bus tracking and digital ticketing. Source: karnataka.com's BMTC overview page, citing official BMTC fare notifications.

**Vayu Vajra** is BMTC's air-conditioned airport express service, connecting Kempegowda International Airport (KIA) with multiple points across the city including Majestic, Shivajinagar, Marathahalli, Hebbal, and Electronic City, running over 250 trips daily as of the source's reporting. This is relevant background for public-transport queries about airport access, since — as of this writing — **Namma Metro does not yet reach the airport** (see below), making Vayu Vajra the primary public-transport option for that route.

**Karnataka Shakti Scheme (free bus travel for women):** under this state scheme, launched 11 June 2023, all women and transgender residents of Karnataka can travel free on **ordinary (non-AC) BMTC buses** by presenting a valid government-issued photo ID — no separate pass or registration is required. As of the source's July 2025 reporting, over 500 crore free tickets had been issued under the scheme statewide, and studies cited (Azim Premji University) found women outnumbering men on several of the city's busiest routes following implementation. This is directly relevant to a public-transport query from a woman asking about bus fares — the assistant should be aware the answer may simply be "free, with photo ID, on ordinary buses" rather than quoting a fare. Source: karnataka.com's BMTC overview, citing the Shakti Scheme's public launch details.

## Namma Metro (BMRCL)

Namma Metro is Bengaluru's rapid transit system, operated by Bangalore Metro Rail Corporation Limited (BMRCL). As of the most recent figures available (April–July 2026 reporting), the network has **3 operational lines** (Purple, Green, and Yellow — the Yellow Line, connecting toward Electronic City, opened 10 August 2025), **83 stations**, and roughly **96.1 km** of operational track. Average daily ridership was reported at approximately **9.16 lakh (916,461)** as of April 2026. Source: Wikipedia's Namma Metro summary page, cross-referenced against independent 2026 metro-guide sites reporting consistent station and line counts.

**Fares:** the current fare system is **distance-based**, calculated by the number of stations travelled. Following a fare reform that took effect **9 February 2025**, the range is reported as **₹10 to ₹90** by some sources and **approximately ₹11 to ₹95** by a more recent (2026) source describing an annual revision — these figures are not perfectly consistent across sources and likely reflect different points in an evolving fare schedule; the assistant should present this as an approximate range rather than a precise current figure, and note that a further fare increase originally announced for 9 February 2026 was reported as **officially suspended before it took effect**. Source: city-rails.com's 2026 Bangalore Metro guide (most recent, most detailed) and metrofare.in's fare calculator page.

**Ticket types:** single-journey QR-code tickets, physical tokens, rechargeable Smart Cards (available for a ₹50 issuance fee, carrying an automatic 5% discount on every journey), the National Common Mobility Card (NCMC), group tickets (for up to ten passengers travelling between the same two stations), and day-pass products (1-day, 3-day, and 5-day unlimited-travel passes). An integrated Metro+Bus day pass is also available for combined travel. Source: metroeasy.com's Namma Metro guide, cross-referenced against trackmymetro.com.

**Operating hours:** generally **5:00 AM to 11:00 PM** on weekdays, with train frequency of roughly 3–15 minutes depending on the line and time of day (independent sources describe this range slightly differently — some cite 10 minutes peak/15 minutes off-peak specifically). Weekend start times may differ slightly; the assistant should advise checking the live Namma Metro app or BMRCL notices for exact timing rather than quoting a single fixed number, since sources are not fully consistent here.

**Airport access:** as of the most recent reporting (July 2026), **Kempegowda International Airport has no operating metro station**. A Blue Line extension toward the airport (from Hebbal) is under construction; until it opens, the practical public-transport option to/from the airport is the BMTC Vayu Vajra bus service described above, or the "Flybus" service referenced from Majestic and Yeshwanthpur, with a typical journey time of roughly 60–75 minutes depending on traffic. Source: trackmymetro.com and city-rails.com, both independently describing the same airport-connectivity gap and construction status.

**Metro-BMTC integration:** the two systems interchange at major points including Majestic, Yeshwanthpur, Banashankari, and Baiyappanahalli, and the integrated day pass mentioned above allows combined travel across both networks.
