# Riverside Clinic — Hours and Scheduling

## Opening Hours
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->
The clinic is open Monday to Friday from nine in the morning to six in the evening,
and Saturday from nine in the morning to one in the afternoon. It is closed on Sundays
and public holidays.

## Rescheduling an Appointment
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->
Appointments can be rescheduled up to two hours before the scheduled time through the
patient portal or by speaking with a staff member. Same-day rescheduling is subject to
availability.

## Billing Enquiries
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->
Billing statements are issued monthly and can be paid online, by phone, or in person
at the front desk. Insurance claims are filed on the patient's behalf when insurance
details are on file.
