# AKGEC — Placements & Training

<!-- source: akgec.ac.in/placements/tp-department, /placements/placement-records, /placements/our-recruiters, akgec.ac.in home page | retrieved 2026-08-01 -->

## Training and Placement Department
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The AKGEC Training and Placement Cell coordinates campus recruitment for students. The Dean of Training and Placement is Dr. Rakesh Srivastava, reachable at tpo@akgec.ac.in. The cell focuses on maximising each student's career prospects and manages the full campus recruitment cycle, from company invitation through to offer rollout. AKGEC publishes a Placement Brochure each year, available on the college placements pages.

## Placement Record
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

More than 2,000 students were placed in the 2023-24 session. Over the years, 213 AKGEC students have appeared in the university merit list and more than 19,000 students have graduated from the college. AKGEC was the first and only college in Uttar Pradesh to receive the Academic Excellence Award for the Best Engineering College in UPTU from the Governor of Uttar Pradesh, in two successive years. Year-wise placement counts, year-wise package figures, and the list of companies visited are published as charts on the Placement Records page.

## Highest Package and Top Offers
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The highest offer received by an AKGEC student is Rs. 1.13 crore per annum. Mr. Deepak Sharma, a final year B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student, was selected by Amazon Dublin at a package of Rs. 1 crore 13 lakh per annum. In addition, 27 AKGEC students received a Rs. 25 lakh annual package at Amazon, one student received Rs. 28.70 lakh at Cloudera, and three students received Rs. 21 lakh each at Walmart Global.

## Recruiters
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Companies that have conducted recruitment at AKGEC and offered jobs to students include Adobe India, Amazon, Infosys, TCS, American Express, Accenture, Lowe's India, Capgemini, Cognizant, Wipro, OLA Electric, PricewaterhouseCoopers, IBM, Birlasoft, Byju's, Motherson Sumi, NIIT, HCL Technologies, Tech Mahindra, L&T Infotech, and Walmart Global, among many others. Students have also been placed in public sector undertakings. The full recruiter list is maintained on the Our Recruiters page.

## Training Programmes
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Special efforts are made to prepare students for industry standards in both technical knowledge and soft skills. The college is part of the Infosys Campus Connect Program, which trains students in line with industry needs. Specialised courses are conducted for CAD packages such as Pro-E. There is a continuous college and industry interface, with interactive seminars on emerging technologies held regularly. The college also runs a Personality Development Program that builds soft skills and self-confidence.

## Industrial Training
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The college helps students undergo industrial training of four to six weeks. This applies to second year Mechanical Engineering students and to third year students of all branches. Students have been placed for industrial training at organisations including BHEL, CMC Noida, Samsung Software, Genpact, HCL, NTPC Dadri, Miracle Technologies, and Birlasoft.

## Placement Eligibility and Process
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: eligibility thresholds and process steps are placeholders pending the official T&P policy document. Replace before any real deployment. -->

Students register with the Training and Placement Cell at the start of the pre-final year through the ERP portal. Typical eligibility requires an aggregate of 60 percent or above with no active carry-over papers, though individual companies set their own criteria which may be higher. A student who accepts an offer above the dream offer threshold is generally withdrawn from further placement drives so that opportunities are distributed across the batch. Drive schedules, shortlists, and results are published on the ERP portal and through department placement coordinators.

## Placement Preparation Timeline
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: timeline is a placeholder pending the official T&P calendar. -->

Aptitude and communication training begins in the second year and continues through the third year. Mock aptitude tests, group discussions, and technical and human resource interview practice sessions are conducted in the semester before the placement season opens. The main campus recruitment season runs from the start of the seventh semester, with pre-placement talks scheduled through the Training and Placement Cell.
