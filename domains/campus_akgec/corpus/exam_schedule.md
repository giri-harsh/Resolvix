# AKGEC — Examination Rules & Attendance

<!-- source: akgec.ac.in/academics/academic-calendar, /central-library, /scholarship, /uptac, aktu.ac.in | retrieved 2026-08-01 -->

## Examination Pattern
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

AKGEC is affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow, and follows the AKTU examination scheme. Assessment across a semester has three components: internal Sessional examinations conducted by the college, the Pre-University Test known as PUT, and the End Semester University Examination conducted by AKTU. Previous question papers for Sessional, PUT, and University examinations, along with model solutions prepared by subject teachers, are maintained in the Central Library question paper bank and in the DSpace institutional repository.

## Sessional Examinations
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: timings and weightage are placeholders pending the official examination circular. Verify against the Academic Office notice before any real deployment. -->

Two Sessional examinations are conducted in each semester, in roughly the sixth week and the twelfth week. Each Sessional carries internal marks that contribute to the sessional component of the AKTU result along with assignments, quizzes, and laboratory work. Sessional exam datesheets are published on the ERP portal and on department notice boards about ten days in advance. A student who misses a Sessional for a documented medical reason may apply to the Head of Department for a make-up test within one week of returning.

## Pre-University Test
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: scheduling detail is a placeholder pending the official examination circular. -->

The Pre-University Test is a full-syllabus mock examination held before the End Semester University Examination. It follows the AKTU paper pattern and duration so that students get realistic practice. PUT performance does not count towards the university result but is used by departments to identify students who need additional academic support before the university exam.

## End Semester University Examination
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The End Semester University Examination is set and conducted by AKTU, not by the college. The datesheet, question papers, and results are released by the university through the AKTU portal. The examination fee of Rs. 7,500 for B.Tech and MCA students, and Rs. 10,000 for M.Tech students, is paid directly to the university as and when demanded, along with the Digital Library fee. The syllabus for every subject is published by the university at aktu.ac.in.

## Attendance Requirement
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: the specific percentage and debarment process are placeholders pending the official attendance policy. -->

Students must maintain a minimum of 75 percent attendance in each subject to be eligible to appear in the End Semester University Examination. Attendance is recorded on the ERP portal and is visible to students and parents. A student falling below 75 percent is issued a shortage notice, and continued shortage results in the student being detained from that subject's university examination. Every student signs an attendance undertaking at the time of admission acknowledging this requirement. Students holding a college scholarship must maintain attendance above 90 percent to keep it.

## Carry Over Papers
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: the carry-over process described here is a placeholder pending the official AKTU ordinance summary. -->

A subject a student fails to clear is called a carry-over paper. Carry-over papers are cleared by appearing in the special carry-over examination conducted by AKTU, usually held after the even semester results. A student carrying more than the permitted number of papers may not be promoted to the next year as per the AKTU promotion ordinance. Scholarship holders lose their scholarship if they have any carry-over paper in any semester. Carry-over examination forms and fees are submitted through the college examination cell within the window announced by the university.

## Admit Card
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: placeholder pending the official examination cell notice. -->

Admit cards for the End Semester University Examination are generated by AKTU and distributed through the college examination cell. An admit card is released only after the student has cleared all outstanding college dues and has met the attendance requirement. Students should collect the admit card from their department office at least three days before the first paper and must carry it along with the college identity card to every examination.

## Results and Revaluation
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: placeholder pending the official examination cell notice. -->

Results are declared by AKTU on the university result portal. A student who wants an answer script rechecked or revalued applies online through the AKTU portal within the window announced with the result, paying the prescribed per-subject fee. Scrutiny checks for totalling and unmarked answers, while revaluation is a fresh evaluation of the script. The college examination cell assists students with the application process.

## Academic Calendar
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The college publishes an academic calendar for each semester, covering the start and end of teaching, examination periods, and vacation dates. The calendar for the current session is available on the Academic Calendar page of the college website. A separate list of declared holidays is also published each year.

## Question Paper Bank and Study Resources
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The AKGEC Central Library maintains a Question Papers and Model Solutions Bank of previous Sessional, PUT, and University examination papers with solutions prepared by subject teachers. These are available in print in the Central Library and electronically in departmental and hostel libraries, and through the open source DSpace institutional repository. The library also provides access to NPTEL, NDL, Coursera, edX, e-ShodhGanga, and e-Pathshala.
