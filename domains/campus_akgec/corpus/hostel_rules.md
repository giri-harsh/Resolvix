# AKGEC — Hostel Rules & Procedures

<!-- source: akgec.ac.in/about/infrastructure-visual-tour/hostel, /uptac, /fee-structure-for-new-students2026-27, /life-akgec/mediclaim | retrieved 2026-08-01 -->

## Hostel Blocks and Occupancy
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

AKGEC has six campus hostels spread over four complexes: the Junior Girls Hostel Complex, the Girls Hostel Complex, the Junior Boys Hostel Complex, and the Senior Boys Hostel Complex. The three girls hostels have double and triple seater rooms and accommodate about 600 students. The Junior Boys Hostel has double occupancy rooms, accommodates about 300 students, and is reserved for first year students. The Senior Boys Hostel Complex has two hostels with single and triple seater accommodation and houses about 600 students.

## Hostel Allotment
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The college has limited hostel facilities for boys, but sufficient hostel accommodation is available for girls. The criterion for allotment of hostel accommodation is the exclusive right of the Director. Hostel allotment is requested at the time of physical reporting for admission, and room type depends on availability in the relevant complex. First year boys are placed in the Junior Boys Hostel, and senior students move to the Senior Boys Hostel Complex.

## Hostel Fee and Security Deposit
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

For B.Tech students admitted in the 2026-27 session, a triple seater room costs Rs. 1,40,000 in total for both boys and girls. This is Rent and Mess Charges of Rs. 1,30,000 plus a refundable Security deposit of Rs. 10,000. MCA boys taking a double seater room pay Rs. 1,50,000 in total. M.Tech boys taking a single seater room pay Rs. 1,60,000 in total, and M.Tech girls taking a double seater pay Rs. 1,50,000. Hostel rent and mess charges cover the academic session. Rates are revised each year and are subject to change by the State Government or the Fee Fixation Committee.

## Hostel Withdrawal and Refund
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

If a student withdraws from the hostel, only the refundable security deposit and the mess charges for unutilised months are refunded, calculated on a pro rata basis. Rent for the period already occupied is not refunded. The security deposit itself is returned after deduction of dues once the student finally leaves the college on completion of the course, through the same No Dues clearance process handled by the Accounts Office.

## Amenities Provided
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Each hostel room is furnished and includes curtains, mattress, pillow, and bed sheets. Every hostel complex has its own mess with a well equipped kitchen and dining area. Hostels have wifi internet, solar water heating systems, a gymnasium, a TV room, a library with magazines and general reading books, and indoor and outdoor sports facilities. Each complex also has a canteen and a provision store for minor daily requirements.

## Mess Facility and Timings
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: placeholder pending the official hostel handbook. Replace with the Chief Warden's circular before any real deployment. -->

Mess charges are included in the hostel rent and mess component and cover four meals a day. Breakfast is served from 7:30 to 9:00 in the morning, lunch from 12:30 to 2:00 in the afternoon, evening snacks and tea from 5:00 to 6:00, and dinner from 8:00 to 9:30 at night. Timings are extended during examination periods as notified by the Warden. A weekly menu is displayed in each dining hall and is reviewed by the Hostel Mess Committee, which includes student representatives. Mess rebate is granted only for a continuous absence of seven days or more, applied for in advance through the Warden.

## Entry Timings and Curfew
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: placeholder pending the official hostel handbook. -->

Hostel gates close at 9:30 at night for all residents, and students must be inside their allotted hostel by this time. Night attendance is taken by the Warden or Assistant Warden after gate closing. Late entry beyond the cutoff requires prior written permission from the Warden and is recorded in the late entry register. Repeated late entry without permission is treated as a disciplinary matter and is reported to the parent or guardian.

## Leave and Outing Permission
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: placeholder pending the official hostel handbook. -->

Hostellers must take prior permission from the Warden before leaving the campus for an overnight stay or for going home. Leave requests are submitted through the hostel leave form, countersigned by the Warden, and the parent or guardian is informed. Day outings on Sundays and holidays require an entry in the movement register at the hostel gate. Students leaving for vacation must record their departure and expected return date with the Warden.

## Visitors
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: placeholder pending the official hostel handbook. -->

Visitors are received only in the designated visitors room at the hostel entrance, and only during visiting hours, which are 4:00 to 7:00 in the evening on Sundays and declared holidays. Visitors are not permitted inside residential floors under any circumstances. Parents and guardians must record their visit in the visitors register and carry photo identification. Male visitors are not permitted in the girls hostel complexes beyond the reception area.

## Prohibited Items and Conduct
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: placeholder pending the official hostel handbook. -->

Cooking appliances such as induction cooktops, heaters, and immersion rods are not permitted in rooms because of fire risk. Alcohol, tobacco products, narcotics, weapons, and pets are strictly prohibited anywhere on the hostel premises. Playing loud music after 10:00 at night is not allowed. Damage to hostel property is recovered from the student's security deposit. Ragging in any form is a criminal offence and results in immediate expulsion from the hostel and referral to the Anti-Ragging Committee.

## Room Change and Vacating
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: placeholder pending the official hostel handbook. -->

Requests for a change of room or roommate are made in writing to the Warden and are considered only after the first month of the session, subject to availability. Students vacating the hostel at the end of the session must hand over the room to the Warden, return the room key and furniture in good condition, and obtain a hostel clearance certificate. The security deposit is not processed without this clearance.

## Medical Facility for Hostellers
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

All students, including hostellers, get a free annual medical check-up and free OPD at Dr. R.S.G. Indo-German Hospital, a fifty bed hospital with ICU, operation theatres, and diagnostic facilities available seven days a week and round the clock. The college has an ambulance for transporting students needing emergency medical treatment. The college has also taken a Group Mediclaim policy of Rs. 50,000 and a Group Accidental policy of Rs. 1,00,000 per student from Bharti AXA General Insurance Company Limited.

## Anti-Ragging in Hostels
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Every student must submit an Anti-Ragging Undertaking online through the national portal at www.antiragging.in. Both the student undertaking and the student and parent undertaking must be signed and submitted to the college at the time of reporting. AKGEC has a constituted Anti-Ragging Committee, and ragging cases can be reported online through the college website. Ragging is a criminal offence and attracts expulsion in addition to legal action.
