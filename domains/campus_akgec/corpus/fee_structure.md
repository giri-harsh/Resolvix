# AKGEC — Fee Structure & Payment FAQ

<!-- source: akgec.ac.in/fee-structure-for-new-students2026-27, /fee-refund-norms, /scholarship, /academics/policy-and-process-for-refund-of-caution-money | retrieved 2026-08-01 -->

## B.Tech First Year Academic Fee
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The academic fee for B.Tech students admitted in the 2026-27 session is Rs. 1,42,156 for the first year. This is made up of Registration Fees Rs. 1,500, Tuition and Development Fees Rs. 1,11,256, Medical Charges Rs. 1,500, Book Bank Fees Rs. 2,900, a one-time Activity Fee of Rs. 10,000, a one-time Career Planning and Placement charge of Rs. 10,000, and refundable Caution Money of Rs. 5,000. Any amount already deposited during UPTAC counselling is deducted from this total. The fee structure is subject to change by the State Government or the Fee Fixation Committee.

## Fee Waiver Scheme Fee
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Students admitted under the Fee Waiver Scheme pay Rs. 41,014 in the first year instead of the full academic fee. This covers Registration Fees Rs. 1,500, Development Fees Rs. 10,114, Medical Charges Rs. 1,500, Book Bank Fees Rs. 2,900, a one-time Activity Fee of Rs. 10,000, a one-time Career Planning and Placement charge of Rs. 10,000, and refundable Caution Money of Rs. 5,000. Tuition fee is waived under this scheme; the other heads still apply.

## M.Tech Fee
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

M.Tech first year fee for 2026-27 is Rs. 1,19,256, payable semester-wise. The first semester instalment is Rs. 63,628 and the second semester instalment is Rs. 55,628. The breakup is Registration Fees Rs. 1,500 in semester one, Tuition and Development Fees of Rs. 55,628 per semester totalling Rs. 1,11,256, Medical Charges Rs. 1,500, and refundable Caution Money of Rs. 5,000. M.Tech students pay Examination Fee of Rs. 10,000 and Digital Library Fee of Rs. 1,000 directly to the university.

## MCA Fee
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The MCA academic fee for the 2026-27 session is published as a detailed PDF sheet on the college fee structure page, and it uses the same heads as the undergraduate programme. A separate reduced sheet applies to MCA students admitted under the Fee Waiver Scheme. MCA students pay Examination Fee of Rs. 7,500 and Digital Library Fee of Rs. 700 directly to the university.

## Hostel Fee
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

For B.Tech students admitted in 2026-27, a triple seater room costs Rs. 1,40,000 in total for boys and Rs. 1,40,000 for girls. This is Rent and Mess Charges of Rs. 1,30,000 plus a refundable Security deposit of Rs. 10,000. For MCA students, a double seater room for boys costs Rs. 1,50,000 in total, made up of Rs. 1,40,000 rent and mess plus Rs. 10,000 security. For M.Tech students, a single seater room for boys costs Rs. 1,60,000 total and a double seater for girls costs Rs. 1,50,000 total. Hostel rates are revised annually and are subject to change by the State Government or Fee Fixation Committee.

## University Fees Paid Directly
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Some fees are not paid to the college. B.Tech and MCA students pay an Examination Fee of Rs. 7,500 and a Digital Library Fee of Rs. 1,000 directly to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University as and when demanded. M.Tech students pay an Examination Fee of Rs. 10,000 and a Digital Library Fee of Rs. 1,000. These amounts are set by the university and are subject to change by the university.

## Fee Refund on Cancellation of Admission
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

If a student cancels admission, the refund follows the AICTE notification and the Government Order passed by the Uttar Pradesh Government. If the refund request is received before the session starts, the entire fee is refunded after deducting Rs. 1,000. If the request is received after the session has started and the seat is not filled by the college until the last date of admissions, only the caution money is refunded. In case of withdrawal from the hostel, only the security deposit and mess charges for unutilised months are refundable, calculated on a pro rata basis.

## Caution Money Refund
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Caution money and the hostel security deposit are refundable after deduction of any dues, but only after the student finally leaves the college on completion of the course. Every student in the final year of their course must fill up a clearance form, also called a No Dues form, and submit it to the Accounts Office before leaving the college. The Accounts Office processes these forms and distributes the caution money cheques at the College Convocation. Students are individually informed by letter and email. Students who cannot attend the Convocation Ceremony may collect their cheque on any other working day during college hours.

## Scholarships and Merit Fee Waivers
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

AKGEC offers a scholarship to students securing an All India Rank up to 25,000 in JEE for admission to B.Tech, and an All India Rank between 1 and 750 in CUET PG for admission to MCA. From the second year onward, for both B.Tech and MCA, the scholarship continues only if the student maintains overall attendance above 90 percent and a CGPA above 7.5 with no carry-over papers in any semester. For M.Tech, scholarships are linked to B.Tech aggregate marks: 25 percent of tuition fee for 65 percent or above, 50 percent for 70 percent or above, 75 percent for 75 percent or above, and 100 percent for 80 percent or above. AKGEC alumni qualify at five percentage points lower in each band.

## Education Loan Documents
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The published fee structure serves as a demand letter from the college and is valid for a bank loan application. Students applying for an education loan can also use the AICTE approval letter, the university affiliation letter, the Information Booklet for the session, and the provisional fee details sheet, all of which are downloadable from the college reporting page.

## Late Payment and Instalments

Semester fee is due within fifteen days of the start of each semester as notified on the ERP portal. A late fee of Rs. 100 per day applies after the due date, capped at Rs. 3,000 per semester. Students facing genuine financial hardship may apply in writing to the Accounts Office for permission to pay in two instalments within the same semester; approval is at the discretion of the Director. Examination admit cards are released only after all outstanding college dues are cleared.
