# AKGEC — Admissions

<!-- source: akgec.ac.in/uptac, /courses-offered, /admissions, /scholarship, /fee-structure-for-new-students2026-27 | retrieved 2026-08-01 -->

## About the College
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Ajay Kumar Garg Engineering College, known as AKGEC, is in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. It was established in 1998 and is affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow, with College Code 027. The college offers a four year undergraduate B.Tech programme in all major engineering branches, a two year postgraduate M.Tech programme in four branches, and a two year MCA programme. The campus is at the 27th Km Milestone, Delhi-Meerut Expressway, P.O. Adhyatmik Nagar, Ghaziabad 201015.

## Courses Offered and Sanctioned Intake
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The total sanctioned B.Tech intake is 1,320 seats. Branch-wise intake is Computer Science and Engineering 180, Information Technology 180, Electronics and Communication Engineering 180, Computer Science 150, Computer Science and Engineering with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning 120, Computer Science and Engineering with Data Science 120, Computer Science and Information Technology 120, Computer Science and Engineering in Hindi 60, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning 60, Mechanical Engineering 60, Electrical and Electronics Engineering 60, and Civil Engineering 30. M.Tech intake is 72 seats in total, with Computer Science and Engineering 18, Electrical and Electronics Engineering 18, Electronics and Communication Engineering 18, and Mechanical Engineering 9. MCA intake is 120 seats.

## Admission Through UPTAC Counselling
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Admission to B.Tech at AKGEC is primarily through Uttar Pradesh Technical Admission Counselling, known as UPTAC, conducted at uptac.samarth.edu.in. Candidates register for UPTAC, fill and lock their choices, and receive a seat allotment letter. A candidate who has been allotted AKGEC and has frozen their choice as one of the courses or branches at AKGEC must then complete physical reporting at the college. Reporting means completing the admission formalities by submitting the required fees and documents at the college, in accordance with the terms stated in the UPTAC allotment letter.

## Documents Required at Reporting
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Candidates must bring the original documents for verification along with their photocopies, in the order specified by the college, when reporting physically. The list of required documents is published on the reporting process page of the college website and is also linked from the UPTAC allotment letter. Candidates should carry the allotment letter itself, along with academic certificates and marksheets, in original.

## Forms to Submit at Reporting
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

During physical reporting, candidates fill out the admission form provided by the institute. In addition, candidates must download, complete by hand, and personally submit several documents to the college authorities along with the admission form. These are the Application Form for Student ID Card, an Undertaking relating to fees, an Undertaking relating to attendance, a Transport Form if college transport is required, and an Anti-Ragging Undertaking from both the student and the parents. The Anti-Ragging Undertaking must be submitted online through the national portal at www.antiragging.in, and both the student undertaking and the student and parent undertaking must be signed and submitted to the college.

## Direct Application to the College
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Apart from UPTAC counselling, candidates can apply directly to AKGEC through the college admissions portal at admissions.akgec.ac.in. The Admission Brochure for the 2026-27 session is published on the college website and sets out the programmes, eligibility, and the application process. The admission helpline number is 8744052891 to 93.

## Hostel Availability at Admission
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The college has limited hostel facilities for boys, but sufficient hostel accommodation is available for girls. The criterion for allotment of hostel accommodation is the exclusive right of the Director. Candidates who want hostel accommodation should indicate this at the time of reporting, and the hostel fee is paid separately from the academic fee.

## Fee Payable at Admission
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The B.Tech first year academic fee for the 2026-27 session is Rs. 1,42,156, and students admitted under the Fee Waiver Scheme pay Rs. 41,014. Any amount already deposited during UPTAC counselling is deducted from the total payable at the college. Detailed fee sheets for B.Tech, B.Tech under the Fee Waiver Scheme, B.Tech lateral entry, MCA, MCA under the Fee Waiver Scheme, and M.Tech are published as separate PDFs on the fee structure page for new students.

## Lateral Entry
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

AKGEC admits diploma holders into the second year of B.Tech through lateral entry, and a separate academic fee sheet is published for lateral entry students admitted in the 2026-27 session. Lateral entry admission is also routed through UPTAC counselling. Fee structure sheets for lateral entry students in the second to fourth year are published on the fee structure page for existing students.

## Scholarship for New Admissions
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

AKGEC offers a scholarship to students securing an All India Rank up to 25,000 in JEE for admission to B.Tech, and an All India Rank between 1 and 750 in CUET PG for admission to MCA. The scholarship is intended to support meritorious students and encourage academic excellence. From the second year onward, for both B.Tech and MCA, the scholarship continues only if the student maintains overall attendance above 90 percent and a CGPA above 7.5 with no carry-over papers in any semester. For M.Tech admission, tuition fee waivers of 25 to 100 percent are linked to the aggregate marks obtained in B.Tech, with AKGEC alumni qualifying at five percentage points lower in each band.

## Approvals and Accreditation
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

AKGEC is approved by AICTE and affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow. AICTE approval letters, the university affiliation letter, NBA accreditation details, NIRF ranking data, and the college's mandatory disclosure are all published on the college website. Students applying for an education loan can use the AICTE approval letter, the affiliation letter, the Information Booklet for the session, and the provisional fee details sheet as supporting documents.
