# AKGEC — Certificates, Documents & General Requests

<!-- source: akgec.ac.in/academics/issuance-of-certificates-for-passout-students, /academics/educational-verification, /grievance-committee, /central-library, /life-akgec/mediclaim, /uptac | retrieved 2026-08-01 -->

## ERP Portal
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

AKGEC runs a student ERP portal at erp.akgec.ac.in. Students use the ERP to check attendance, internal marks, examination datesheets, fee status, and college notices. Login credentials are issued at the time of admission. Most academic information a student needs day to day is published on the ERP rather than on the public website.

## Certificates for Passout Students
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The college issues four certificates to students who have completed their course: the Character Certificate, the Transfer Certificate, the Migration Certificate, and the Degree Certificate. Each has its own list of documents that must be submitted at the college. Students must collect their Transfer Certificate and Character Certificate within one month from the date of application, whether the application was made online or offline. A student who fails to collect within that period may collect in the next one month after paying a fine of Rs. 200. Certificates not collected within two months from the date of application are cancelled, and the student must apply again, in which case both certificates can be obtained on payment of Rs. 500.

## Bonafide Certificate
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: placeholder pending the official Academic Section process note. -->

A bonafide certificate confirming that a student is currently enrolled is issued by the Academic Section. The student submits a written application through their Head of Department stating the purpose, such as a passport application, bank account opening, education loan, or scholarship claim. The certificate is normally issued within three working days of the application being forwarded. There is no charge for the first two bonafide certificates in an academic year.

## Marksheets and Transcripts
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: placeholder pending the official examination cell process note. -->

Semester marksheets are issued by AKTU and distributed to students through the college examination cell. A student who needs a consolidated transcript for higher studies or a visa application applies through the college examination cell, which forwards the request to the university. Duplicate marksheets are issued by AKTU on payment of the university's prescribed fee and require an affidavit and a police report in case of loss.

## Educational Verification
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

Employers, universities, and verification agencies that need to verify the educational credentials of an AKGEC student or alumnus can do so through the Educational Verification page on the college website, which sets out the process the college follows for such requests.

## Student Identity Card
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

At the time of admission every student fills an Application Form for Student ID Card, which is provided at physical reporting. The identity card doubles as the Library cum Identity Card and is scanned at the Central Library entrance to record entry. Students must carry the identity card on campus and to every examination.

## Library Services
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The AKGEC Library System has a Central Library, nine departmental libraries, and five hostel libraries. The Central Library is in the Administrative Block, spread over 1,465 square metres with seating for 344 users, and includes a reading hall, study cubicles, a digital library, and a faculty reading room. It runs KOHA open source library management software with a web based online public access catalogue, a DSpace institutional repository, and membership of DELNET and the National Digital Library. All students, faculty, and staff can use the library on taking library membership. The library is open to an average of 350 users on a regular working day and is under camera surveillance.

## Mediclaim and Medical Facility
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

All students receive a free medical check-up and free OPD at Dr. R.S.G. Indo-German Hospital, a fifty bed hospital with ICU, operation theatres, and diagnostic facilities available seven days a week and round the clock. The college has an ambulance for emergency transport. The college has also taken a Group Mediclaim policy of Rs. 50,000 and a Group Accidental policy of Rs. 1,00,000 per student from Bharti AXA General Insurance Company Limited. For a non-cashless claim, the student submits the reimbursement documents listed on the Mediclaim page of the college website.

## Transport
<!-- provenance: synthetic -->

<!-- SYNTHETIC: placeholder pending the official transport circular. -->

The college runs bus routes covering Ghaziabad, Noida, Delhi, and Meerut. Students who need college transport fill the Transport Form at the time of admission and pay the transport fee separately from the academic fee, based on the route selected. Route lists, stops, and timings are published on the ERP portal before the start of each session. A student wanting to change route or discontinue transport applies in writing to the Transport Office.

## Grievance Redressal
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The college has a Student Grievance Redressal Committee for grievances that are not resolved through the normal channels. A student should contact any member of the committee, preferably in writing, so that suitable remedial action can be initiated. Grievances can also be registered online at www.akgec.ac.in. Anonymous or unnamed grievances without proper details are not entertained. A separate Grievance Redressal Committee exists for faculty and staff.

## Anti-Ragging Reporting
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

AKGEC has a constituted Anti-Ragging Committee, and ragging cases can be reported online through the college website. Every student must also submit an Anti-Ragging Undertaking through the national portal at www.antiragging.in, with both the student undertaking and the student and parent undertaking signed and submitted to the college at the time of reporting.

## Internal Complaints Committee
<!-- provenance: sourced -->

The college has an Internal Complaints Committee constituted to handle complaints of sexual harassment. Details of the committee and its members are published on the Internal Complaints Committee page of the college website.
